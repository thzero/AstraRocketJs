# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind, be constructive, and assume good faith. We're all here to build cool
rocketry software together — treat people the way you'd want to be treated.

## What we're going for

- Be respectful, even when you disagree. Critique ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that helps, and take feedback gracefully.
- Assume the best about others; ask before you assume the worst.
- Own your mistakes, apologize, and move on.

## Not okay

Harassment, insults, personal or discriminatory attacks, sexualized comments or
advances, sharing someone's private info, or generally being a jerk. If it would
get you side-eyed at a decent workplace, don't do it here.

This applies everywhere the project lives — issues, PRs, discussions, chat — and
whenever you're representing the project in public.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers via email.
We'll look into it promptly and fairly, and we'll keep your report confidential.

Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a quiet word, to a warning,
to a temporary or permanent ban. We aim to be fair and proportionate.
